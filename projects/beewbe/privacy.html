Privacy policy

Your pet has a home. Your data does too.

Updated 24 October 2026

beewbe works offline, without an account, advertising or tracking. Your game stays on your device.

This policy covers the beewbe app, its widget, this website and messages sent to the developer, Vlas Laptev.

01What is stored

Your pet’s name and appearance, language, progress, collection and paintings are stored locally. The app and widget share this save. beewbe does not send it to the developer and has no analytics or advertising SDKs.

02Retention and deletion

Data is kept while beewbe is installed. To erase local data, delete beewbe in iPhone Settings. Offloading the app or removing its widget keeps the save.

03Device backups

Your save may be included in device backups managed by Apple or your computer. Restoring a backup may restore the save. Manage these copies in your device’s backup settings.

04TestFlight and feedback

During TestFlight testing, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Feedback you send may include screenshots, comments, device details and, depending on how you joined, your name or email address. This information is used to investigate problems and improve beewbe; it is not used for advertising or shared with other third parties. Apple controls its own collection and retention.

05Email, feedback and your choices

If you email Vlas Laptev, your email address, message and any attachments are used to respond and provide support. Messages and copies of beta feedback are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request or investigate the issue, or where legally required. To ask about, access, correct or delete data held by the developer, email the address below. The developer cannot access the game save on your device or directly erase data held by Apple. You can stop TestFlight testing in TestFlight.

vlas.developer@icloud.com
