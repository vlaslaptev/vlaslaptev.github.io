People you trust. Choices you make.

Private by default.
Shared by choice.

Your personal diary works without registration. Backups and partner access are separate choices, ready when you want them.

On your device.

Your diary is stored locally in encrypted form. Keep recording your days offline, with an optional app lock for another layer of privacy.

On your terms.

Choose the observations and date range a partner can see. Preview them before sending an invitation, and revoke access when you need to. Sharing is end-to-end encrypted.

A way back.

Turn on encrypted backups if you want a recovery option. Save your recovery code separately so you can restore your diary on another device.

Revoking access stops future sharing. It cannot erase copies a recipient has already saved.

Your observations, over time

A little history.
A clearer picture.

See your observed cycle lengths, period duration and how they vary. Your history grows from the entries you actually make.

Export your diary as a PDF when you want a copy to keep or bring to a conversation with your doctor.

Calendar predictions are estimates. Garnet is not intended for diagnosis, treatment or contraception.
